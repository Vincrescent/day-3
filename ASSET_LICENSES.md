## Notes

- **Mars GLB**: No official NASA GLB model was available at time of download. Mars uses a procedural texture upgraded with the included 2K texture map.
- **Sun GLB**: NASA only provides USDZ format. The Sun uses a procedural emissive sphere with the included texture.
- **All NASA assets** are created by the United States Government and are not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. §105). They are free for any use worldwide.
- **NASA media usage**: Per NASA's media usage guidelines, NASA material is not used to state or imply endorsement by NASA.
